# Privacy Policy: AudioWizard

> Last updated: 26 October 2026

AudioWizard reads text aloud inside your editor. It has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry, and Dr. OpenSource runs no servers for it.

## What leaves your machine

**With the default neural voices (`audiowizard.engine: neural`)**, the text being read is sent, one sentence at a time, to Microsoft's Edge text-to-speech service (`speech.platform.bing.com`) to turn it into audio. Only the sentences you ask AudioWizard to read are sent: the file, selection or clipboard text you chose. Nothing else from your project, file paths or settings is included. Microsoft's own privacy terms apply to that service.

**With system voices (`audiowizard.engine: system`)**, speech is generated by your operating system. Nothing leaves your machine.

If Microsoft's service can't be reached, AudioWizard falls back to your system voice for that sentence.

## What AudioWizard reads

- The active file, your selection, or your clipboard, only when you run a read command.
- **Ctrl+Alt+C outside the text editor** first runs the editor's normal Copy command, so that text you selected in a chat panel lands on the clipboard, and then reads the clipboard. This replaces what was on your clipboard with your selection, just like pressing Ctrl+C.

## What AudioWizard stores

Only your settings (voice, speed, engine), in your editor's normal settings. Audio is kept in memory while it plays and is not written to disk.
